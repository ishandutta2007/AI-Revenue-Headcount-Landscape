### 2025–2026 AI Stack Revenue, Headcount & Revenue Per Employee

| AI Sector | 2025 Revenue | 2026 Estimated Revenue | Jobs Created Till Now | Revenue Per Employee (2026) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **AI Agents** *(Autonomous Applications)* | $7.60B | $10.90B | 1,100,000 | $9,909 |
| **Foundation Models (API)** *(Token & Cloud Inference)* | $10.60B | $12.00B | 9,500 | $1,263,158 |
| **Foundation Models (Non-API)** *(Enterprise Seats, Coding & Consumer Apps)* | $18.50B | $55.00B | 5,500 | $10,000,000 |
| **AI IaaS** *(Cloud Infrastructure)* | $62.66B | $102.97B | 500,000 | $205,940 |
| **AI Compute (Dedicated)** *(Pure AI Accelerators & GPUs)* | $43.41B | $47.43B | 140,000 | $338,786 |
| **AI Compute (Broader Chipsets)** *(AI-Capable CPUs, NPUs, ASICs & HBM)* | $94.44B | $121.73B | 210,000 | $579,667 |
| **AI Data Centers** *(Physical Facilities & Power/Cooling)* | $147.30B | $180.60B | 145,000 | $1,245,517 |

#### Notes & Methodology
* **Foundation Models (API vs. Non-API):** API tracks standalone token and model licensing; Non-API captures enterprise seats, coding tools, and consumer subscriptions across frontier labs.
* **AI Compute (Dedicated vs. Broader):** Dedicated covers pure-play AI accelerators/GPUs; Broader includes AI-capable CPUs, edge NPUs, custom ASICs, and High-Bandwidth Memory (HBM).
* **AI Data Centers:** Headcount reflects direct operational and specialized facility roles added during the 2025–2026 buildout, excluding indirect construction and supply-chain workers.
* **Revenue Per Employee:** Calculated strictly as `2026 Estimated Revenue / Jobs Created Till Now`.
